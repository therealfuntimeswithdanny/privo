About Privo

Privo is a simple link shortener that doesn't track you. No account needed, no click analytics, no ads. Paste a link, get a short one, share it.

How it works

  1. Paste a link on the home page, and optionally choose your own shortcode.
  2. We read the page's public details (its title, description, and preview image) so the short link looks like the page it points to when you share it.
  3. Share your short link, for example privo.fyi/44hf4.
  4. Visitors see a quick safety check that shows exactly where the link goes, then continue to the destination.

What makes it different

No tracking

No click analytics, no fingerprinting, no tracking cookies. A short link is just a redirect.

Safety check

Before anyone is redirected, they see the full destination and can proceed or report the link.

Real previews

Short links carry the destination's title, description, and image, so previews in social and messaging apps look right.

Built on Cloudflare

Everything runs on Cloudflare's edge network, which keeps Privo fast and reliable.

Use the safety check on your own site

Any website can send its visitors through Privo's safety page without creating a short link. Link to privo.fyi/p/stop with these parameters:

Parameter What it is
url The destination. Must start with http:// or https://, and be URL-encoded.
viaPrivo Set to false when linking directly.
sourceSiteName Your site's name, shown to the visitor.
sourceSiteUrl Your site's address, also http(s) only.

For example:

https://privo.fyi/p/stop ?url=https%3A%2F%2Fexample.com &viaPrivo=false &sourceSiteName=Coffee%20%26%20Code &sourceSiteUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fcoffeencode.cc

If any of the required parameters is missing or invalid, the page explains what's wrong and doesn't redirect anyone.

Your privacy

We store the link you submit, its destination, and the destination's public preview details. We don't store your IP address, and we don't record who opens a link. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Report a link

If a short link points somewhere harmful, tell us at abuse@privo.fyi, or use the report link on the safety page. We review every report.
