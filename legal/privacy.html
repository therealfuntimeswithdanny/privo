Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who We Are

Privo ("the Service") is a privacy-focused URL shortener operated by The Coffee and Code Publication ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Our entire infrastructure runs on the Cloudflare Developer Platform, which allows us to operate a fast, reliable service while keeping the cost low.

2. Information We Collect

Anonymous Link Creation (No Account)

When you shorten a link without signing in, we store only:

  • The original URL you submitted
  • The generated shortcode
  • An optional custom shortcode (if you provided one)
  • The date and time the link was created
  • The destination page's public title, description, site name, and preview image address, read automatically when the link is created (see Section 4)
  • A copy of the destination's preview image, if it has one

We do not store your IP address, browser fingerprint, device information, or any personally identifiable information for anonymous link creation.

Privo Cloud Accounts (Sign in with Google)

Privo Cloud accounts are created by signing in with your Google Account. When you do, we ask Google only for the information covered by two OAuth scopes: userinfo.email (your primary Google Account email address) and userinfo.profile (the basic profile information you have made available on your Google Account). We receive and store:

  • Your email address — your primary Google Account email address, which serves as your user ID. Stored in a Cloudflare D1 SQL database.
  • Your name — as it appears on your Google Account profile. Used to display your account profile within Privo.
  • Your profile photo — the image address of your Google Account profile picture. Used only to display your avatar within Privo.
  • Your links — stored in a separate Cloudflare D1 database, associated with your account
  • Uploaded Media — images you uploaded for custom SEO tags

Google handles the sign-in process itself. We never see or store your Google Account password, and we do not request access to any other part of your Google Account — no contacts, files, calendars, or activity. We use the information above only to identify your account and display your profile in the Service, and we do not use it to serve advertising. Our use of this data complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What We Never Collect

  • Click analytics or link visit statistics
  • IP addresses of visitors who follow shortened links
  • Browser fingerprints or device identifiers
  • Cookies for tracking or advertising purposes
  • Your Google Account password or credentials
  • Location data
  • Social media profiles or demographic information

3. How We Store Your Data

Account Database

Email addresses (used as user IDs), names, and profile photo addresses are stored in a Cloudflare D1 SQL database. This database is separate from link data and encrypted at rest.

Links Database

Shortened URLs, their destinations, and their preview details (title, description, and image address) are stored in a different Cloudflare D1 database, isolated from account information.

Preview Images

Copies of preview images are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage, separate from both databases, and served from privo.fyi.

Safety Page

The page shown before a redirect runs in your browser. We do not keep a record of which links you open. The page asks our server to check the destination for known threats; only the result is cached, briefly, under a hashed key.

Infrastructure

All Privo services run on Cloudflare's global edge network. We do not operate our own servers or use third-party hosting.

Email

Transactional emails are sent via Cloudflare Email Sending. All emails originate exclusively from @privo.fyi addresses.

4. Link Previews and the Safety Page

When you shorten a link

When you create a short link, our servers make a single request to the destination page to read the information it makes publicly available: its title, description, site name, and preview image address (from its Open Graph, Twitter Card, and standard HTML tags). We only read what the page serves to an anonymous visitor. If the page has a preview image, we also download a copy (up to 5 MB, PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, or AVIF only) and store it.

The request identifies itself as PrivoBot and comes from Cloudflare's network, not from your device. We do not pass your IP address or any other information about you to the destination.

Safety check

Before a short link is created, we check its destination against Google Web Risk, a list of known malware, phishing and unwanted-software sites maintained by Google. We send Google only short, partial fingerprints (hash prefixes) of the destination address, never the full URL. Google may see our server's request details as part of that call. If the destination is on the list, the link is not created. If the check can't be completed, the link is created and marked as not checked. Existing links are re-checked every 12 hours, and a link that is later flagged stops redirecting.

A list match means a site is known to be harmful, not that unlisted sites are safe. Always check where a link is going.

The safety page also checks its destination when it opens, including links sent to it by other websites. The destination is sent to our server, which checks it with Google Web Risk using hash prefixes as above. We don't store the address; only the result is cached for up to an hour under a hashed key.

What a short link shows

A short link's page carries the destination's title, description, and preview image so that previews in social apps, messaging apps, and search engines look like the destination. This information is public: anyone who has the short link can see it.

The safety page

When someone opens a short link, they are shown a safety page that states where the link goes and sends them on after 20 seconds, or immediately if they choose to proceed. The page runs in the visitor's browser. We do not track which links people open and we do not store visitor information.

Other websites may also link to the safety page directly. In that case the destination and the name of the sending site come from the address of the link, are shown in the visitor's browser, and are not stored by us.

Third-party resources

Our pages, including the safety page, load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome. When your browser loads these, those providers receive standard request information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not control or receive that data.

Site owners

If you run a website and want a stored preview of your page removed, contact support@privo.fyi.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data we collect solely to:

  • Provide the link shortening and redirection service
  • Generate link previews that match the destination (titles, descriptions, and images)
  • Identify your account when you sign in with Google
  • Display your name and profile photo within your Privo Cloud account
  • Manage your account (if you create one)
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., sign-in and account notices)
  • Respond to support requests or abuse reports
  • Enforce our Terms of Service

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Ever.

6. Email Communications

All emails sent by Privo originate from @privo.fyi addresses via Cloudflare Email Sending. We send emails only when necessary:

  • Account sign-in and security notices
  • Important service announcements
  • Responses to your support inquiries

We do not send marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional content unless you explicitly opt in (and we currently offer no marketing opt-in).

Phishing warning: If you receive an email claiming to be from Privo that asks for credentials or directs you to a suspicious link, please report it to abuse@privo.fyi.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information. We may disclose limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or court order, we may disclose the minimal information necessary to comply.
  • Abuse prevention: If we detect links to illegal content, we may report relevant information to appropriate authorities or abuse prevention services.
  • Service providers: Cloudflare (including D1 and R2) processes data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider, bound by their own privacy commitments and our instructions.
  • Google Sign-In: When you sign in with Google, Google processes the authentication and provides us with your email address, name, and profile photo, as described in Section 2. Google is a processor in this exchange; we do not share your Privo data with Google, and we do not use Google sign-in data for advertising. Our handling of this data follows Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Google Web Risk: receives a short hash prefix of each destination when a link is created (see Section 4).

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide the Service:

Data Type Retention Period
Account email address, name, and profile photo Until you delete your account
Shortened links (anonymous) Until link removal or abuse detection
Shortened links (account) Until you delete the link or your account
Link previews (title, description, image copy) Until the link is removed
Support emails Deleted upon request
Abuse reports Forever

When you delete your account, your email address, name, profile photo reference, and associated links, including their stored previews, are removed from our databases and storage. Anonymous links persist until they are removed for abuse or at our discretion. Signing out of Privo does not delete your account; you can request deletion at any time via support@privo.fyi.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update your account details (your name and photo follow your Google Account profile; you can update them there)
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your links before account deletion
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing (though we process minimal data by design)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@privo.fyi.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Cloudflare D1 databases are encrypted at rest
  • Account and link databases are logically separated
  • Sign-in is delegated to Google OAuth; we never store or handle your Google password
  • Preview images are type-checked and size-limited before storage, and served with restrictive security headers
  • Access to production databases is restricted and logged
  • We regularly review our security practices

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Third-Party Links

Shortened links created with Privo may direct to third-party websites. The safety page shows you the destination before you are redirected, but we cannot verify that a destination is authentic or safe. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any website you visit, and to report suspicious links to abuse@privo.fyi.

12. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may notify users via email (for account holders) or a notice on the Service.

14. Contact Us
